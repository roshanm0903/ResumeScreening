D1 / RECRUITMENT AT VOLUME



Recruitment screening decision brief
Decision for the four month seasonal hiring launch


Recommendation. Protect interview time from unsuitable candidates and move qualified candidates promptly to a
confirmed booking. Automatically reject confirmed mandatory-check failures and candidates below the role-specific LLM
score threshold. Progress the rest to scheduling. Resolve missing inputs and scheduling exceptions through WhatsApp;
reserve human review for unresolved or contested cases and required interventions.

Protect interview time and retain qualified candidates
Unsuitable candidates consume panel time; qualified candidates can slip through before an interview is booked. Delayed
contact is a major driver of candidate loss in this case. Screening mistakes are another risk, whose size needs
validation. Recruiter capacity and scheduling effort contribute to these outcomes. Ladders reported a 7.4 second initial scan
[1]; Cronofy found 42% had left a process over scheduling delays [2].

Why batch. Immediate invitations can use scarce panel slots before stronger applications arrive. Pool applicants by
requisition, prioritize higher passing score bands, and use deadline order within each band. A 12 hour cadence is a
provisional MVP choice: it allows comparison while capping planned batch wait at 12 hours, leaving up to 36 hours for
clarification and delivery retries before the 48 hour outreach limit. Batching does not change rejection thresholds; a full
calendar is a capacity issue. Start with a fixed customer setting, then test shorter or longer intervals against booking quality,
panel effort and SLA breaches. Never extend an applicant's original deadline.

Recruiter journey
Open the requisition, select the role template and approve job-specific criteria. Add the first-round panel; the agent fetches
availability. After publication, monitor scores, rejection rates, outreach deadlines and bookings. Act only on escalations. On
role closure, stop new invitations and notify remaining applicants.

Applicant and candidate journey
Apply on mobile, upload a resume, provide role requirements and select a contact channel. Receive a decision or
clarification within 48 hours. Confirm missing inputs in WhatsApp; if selected, choose one of three slots or request
alternatives. Receive confirmation and reminders, reschedule if needed, and request reasons or human review without an
employee login.

Approach and scope. Use explicit requirements plus role-specific LLM scoring. Reject below the configured threshold; do
not rank within score bands. Assume inbound applications and structured answers already exist. WhatsApp is the first
exception-resolution route; SMS or another approved channel is the fallback.

Launch controls and success metrics
Measurement accountability and staged rollout


Success metrics and guardrails
North star: Qualified candidates with a confirmed first-round interview booking each week; count once per requisition
against approved job criteria.
Business: 1 Time to fill a position; 2 recruiter hours per confirmed booking, including exceptions; 3 cost per qualified
booking, including processing, messaging and human effort.
Performance: 1 Meaningful outreach within 48 hours, excluding generic acknowledgements; 2 invitation-to-booking
conversion; 3 WhatsApp exceptions resolved without human intervention.
Guardrails: 1 Reduce unsuitable interview referrals; pilot ceiling of 2% qualified candidates incorrectly excluded, with
independent sampling and fairness checks; 2 prevent conflicting or duplicate bookings and track system-caused changes; 3
use approved thresholds, retain decision evidence and a review path, and escalate unresolved cases at 36 hours.


Country approval and decision evidence
Employment counsel and the privacy lead approve each market: India hiring and privacy requirements; US federal, state
and local hiring, accessibility and automated-tool duties; Germany GDPR, applicable EU AI Act duties and works council
involvement. Confirm notices, data handling and candidate rights before launch.

Talent acquisition owns versioned rules and their job-related reasons. Redact names; decide further proxy controls during
design. Keep evidence, policy/model/prompt versions, overrides, delivery and tool results with access and retention controls.
Treat resumes as untrusted data; test injection and extraction failures.

Make LLM scoring robust before launch
Use role-specific anchored ratings and source-linked evidence; compute the weighted total deterministically. Test scoring
stability, transferable experience, resume formatting, keyword stuffing and prompt injection. Back-test known good hires,
then independently label samples across all bands to measure false positives and false negatives. Version the rubric,
threshold, model and prompt; revalidate changes before activation.
